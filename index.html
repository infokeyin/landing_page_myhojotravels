About Us

A Karnal-based travel team that treats your trip like our own.

At MyHoJo Travels (My Holiday My Journey), we believe every trip — whether it's a long-awaited family vacation, a quiet honeymoon, a spiritual yatra or a first-time international tour — should feel memorable, comfortable, and budget-friendly.

We provide complete travel solutions under one roof: flights, hotels, holiday packages, visa assistance, train and bus tickets, taxi services, and on-trip support. Our goal is simple — help you explore your dream destinations without worrying about planning, pricing or paperwork.

With a focus on affordable pricing, personalized service, and round-the-clock support, we design travel packages that suit every budget and style. Mountains, beaches, religious destinations or international escapes — we turn your travel dreams into unforgettable memories.
